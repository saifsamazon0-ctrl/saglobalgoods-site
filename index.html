About us

Built on traceability and documented supply.

SA Global Goods Ltd purchases and distributes products in high-demand categories, where clear paperwork and supply chain transparency are essential.

We source exclusively from trusted UK and EU suppliers, with a full commercial invoice on every order. Each inventory batch is carefully checked for an exact listing match, perfect packaging condition and long shelf-life dates, before being labelled and palletised at our UK prep centre for marketplace distribution.

We are a consistent, high-volume buyer looking to build direct, long-term partnerships with brand owners and authorised distributors, based on recurring orders, clear communication and mutual growth.

Our process

From order to delivery in four steps.

1

Sourcing

We place purchase orders with verified UK and EU wholesalers, distributors and brand owners, with an invoice on every order.

2

Quality check

Incoming stock is checked against the listing: pack size, box condition and dates.

3

Prep

Units are barcoded, packaged and palletised at our UK prep centre.

4

Distribution

Stock is distributed across the UK for fast nationwide delivery, with EU marketplaces planned.
